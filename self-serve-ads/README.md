# Self-serve ads

A clickable prototype of self-serve ads with approval at the account level. Once an account is approved, it can promote any fundraiser it runs, including new ones. The bar at the top switches between the open options, so the ads team and engineering can compare them in one place.

Live: https://svahora.github.io/lg/self-serve-ads/

What to try:
- **Season:** Standard, Winter (35-day feature) or Ramadan. Packages and prices change with it.
- **Account:** New to ads, Ran 1-week ads, or Pre-approved. Changes what the dashboard and fundraiser page offer.
- **Gold & Silver:** A) request, review, then pay · B) account manager only · C) live slot count with sold out.
- **Engineering notes:** shows build implications on every screen, including the two ways to approve by account.
- **Creator to admin loop:** request Gold under option A, switch to Admin, choose "Offer Silver", then open Creator, Email and pay.
- **New creator:** on the landing page answer "No", register a link, then approve it in Admin, Interest list.
- **1-week graduate:** request approval from the dashboard, then approve it in Admin, Approved accounts.

All names, amounts and prices are sample data.
