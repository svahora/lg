# Funboarding

A clickable prototype of onboarding and compliance for new fundraisers. It walks the path from sign-up to a live campaign and a payout account, side by side with what the compliance team sees, so anyone can experience the flow without reading the spec.

Live: https://svahora.github.io/lg/funboarding/

What to try:
- Switch the applicant between Organization and Person at the top. Person skips the organization steps.
- In step 4 (Risk form), change the answers and watch the live score in the Admin view, which opens on its own there.
- In step 5, pick the check result: Low, Medium, High or Declined.
- In steps 6 to 8, switch verification between In-house and With vendor:
  - With vendor, try the company registry "Not reachable" and the vendor result "Low result".
  - In-house, a Low result goes live on submitting documents with payouts held. Medium and High wait as a draft until you set the review to Approved.
- In step 10, add a payout account by hand (with a bank statement) or by connecting a bank.
- Open the Path map to see every route at once and jump to any step.

Tags marked "to confirm" or "to discuss" are open questions, not decisions. The vendor and internal tools have neutral names, and all people, organizations and amounts are sample data.
