# FunDetails

A phone-first workspace for running one fundraiser, opened from the dashboard (the list of all your fundraisers). Home leads with a coach's next steps and a grid of six areas (Page, Supporters, Money, Grow, Insights, Team). The coach helps create the fundraiser on the page itself, suggests donation options from the story, and drafts messages and thank-yous in the owner's words.

Live: https://svahora.github.io/lg/fundetails/

What to try:
- **Scope:** Today shows only what exists now. Must, Should and Could add features by priority. The feature list jumps to each one and explains it.
- **Create a fundraiser:** start from Your fundraisers and build the page section by section with the coach, or choose Do it myself.
- **Offerings:** open Page, then Offerings, and see the coach's suggestion with a live donor preview.
- **Supporters:** reach out one by one, then thank everyone in batches.
- **Status:** tap the fundraiser card on Home to go live, pause, resume or end early.
- **Season:** switch to In Ramadan under Could to see the leaderboard and Ramadan colors.
- **Notes:** turn on to see what each screen is for and what exists today.

All names, amounts and fundraisers are sample data.
